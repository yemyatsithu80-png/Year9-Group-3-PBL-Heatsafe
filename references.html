References

These trusted organisations publish up-to-date guidance on heat waves and heat-related illness. Visit them to learn more.

World Health Organization (WHO): Heat and health

Facts on how heat affects the body, who is most at risk, and how communities can prepare.

https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health

U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC): Heat and Health

Plain-language advice on heat illness, warning signs, and staying cool.

https://www.cdc.gov/heat-health/

U.S. National Weather Service: Heat Safety

Tips for staying safe in the heat, including for children, pets, and outdoor workers.

https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat

World Meteorological Organization (WMO)

Global weather and climate information, including reports on extreme heat.

https://wmo.int/
